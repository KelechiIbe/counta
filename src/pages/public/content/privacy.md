# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Who we are

TaxStreem Technologies Limited ("TaxStreem", "we"), RC 8807119, of Silverwood Court 2, DPKAY Estate, Orchid Road, Lekki, Lagos, provides Counta and TaxStreem's e-invoicing services. This notice explains how we handle personal data in them, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("the Act").

Questions about personal data go to our Data Protection Officer at [dpo@taxstreem.com](mailto:dpo@taxstreem.com).

## Our two roles

For your own account and your business's account with us, TaxStreem decides how personal data is used and is its data controller.

Your business also records people in Counta: customers on invoices, suppliers on bills, members of your team. For that data, your business is the controller, and TaxStreem processes it on your behalf and on your instructions. A person who wants to see or change such data should ask the business that recorded it, and we help that business answer.

## What we collect

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider), sign-in records |
| Business | Business name, Tax ID, RC number, address, directors' names for financial statements, bank details for payouts |
| NRS credentials | Your NRS Business ID and Service ID; your API key, API secret and crypto keys, encrypted with keys in a hardware security module, with only their last four characters readable |
| Identity checks | The result of a check, with only the last four digits of a NIN; a NIN slip you upload, which only the business owner can open |
| Transactions | Sales, invoices, receipts, bills, payments, stock and bank records, with the names, contact details, Tax IDs and addresses of your customers and suppliers |
| Conversations | Messages with our support team and with Martina, Counta's assistant, by chat, WhatsApp or voice when you choose them |
| Technical | Device and browser details, IP addresses, error logs and notification settings |

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Counta keeps your session and display preferences in your browser's own storage.

## Transfers outside Nigeria

Counta and its main database run in Google Cloud in the United States, and a live copy of the database is kept on servers in Lagos. Some of our other providers also work from outside Nigeria. We transfer personal data abroad only where the Act allows it, relying on the protections in our providers' contracts and on their security certifications.

## How long we keep it

We keep invoices, credit notes, their NRS records and other accounting records for at least six years after the end of the year they relate to, as Nigerian tax law requires. We keep account data while your account is open, and afterwards only as long as we need it to settle claims or meet a legal duty.

## How we protect it

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest, with NRS credentials held under keys in a hardware security module.
- Each business's records are open only to its own members, under rules the database enforces.
- TaxStreem staff reach administrative tools only with a code from an authenticator app.
- Owners, and anyone who can change a business's settings or NRS keys, sign in to Counta with a code from an authenticator app as well as a password.
- We watch the service continuously and report personal data breaches to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours where the Act requires, and tell the people affected without undue delay.

## Your rights

Under the Act you may:

- ask for a copy of your personal data;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data deleted where we no longer need it or have no lawful basis to keep it;
- restrict or object to some uses;
- receive data you gave us in a common electronic format;
- withdraw consent you gave, without affecting what happened before;
- refuse to be subject to a decision with legal effect made only by automated means.

Write to [dpo@taxstreem.com](mailto:dpo@taxstreem.com) to use any of these rights. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission](https://ndpc.gov.ng).

## Children

Counta is for businesses and the people who work in them. It is not meant for anyone under 18.
